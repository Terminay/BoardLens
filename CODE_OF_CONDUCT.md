# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Everyone participating in the rigspec community is expected to help make it a respectful, constructive environment.

Examples of behavior that contributes to this include:

- Being considerate and welcoming to people with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Giving and receiving technical feedback respectfully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code, not on people.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them constructively.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report behavior that violates this code privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a participant when needed.

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and other project-related communication.
